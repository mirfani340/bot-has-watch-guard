# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly
- Focus feedback on code and behavior, not people

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Hate speech or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block contributors who violate this code.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact project maintainers privately.
